application opens and provides all shared services (navigation, commands,
security, focus, controls, lifecycle) to every content page.

(12 Zustand stores, 44 files, hitanshu/experimental branch).

        PARTIAL  = concept/mechanism exists but logic incomplete
        GAP      = not implemented, needs to be built
